## C-2026-09-30 | Onset detector fires at the season boundary in 10 of 27 seasons (§5.1.2, C-03, P1) | Author | Disclosure + secondary readout; no ledger value changed

**Nature of the error.** The registered onset rule — first week in MMWR 40–20 at which SMA4 − SMA12 of national weighted ILI exceeds 0.2 pp — does not require the divergence to *cross* 0.2 inside the season window. Recomputed season by season on CDC ILINet (Delphi Epidata mirror, latest-issue vintage, pulled 2026-09-30), the detector fires on the **first eligible week (MMWR 40) in 10 of the 27 paired seasons**: 2011-12, 2013-14, 2014-15, 2016-17, 2017-18, 2018-19, 2019-20, 2022-23, 2023-24, 2025-26. In eight of those the divergence was already above 0.2 at week 40 and never fell below it before the level comparator fired, so the recorded onset week is the calendar boundary, not a signal event. The pattern is recent: 8 of the last 12 seasons. The level comparator (baseline = mean of the season's first four weeks, +1.0 pp) cannot fire before week 44 by construction, so a boundary firing guarantees the divergence a head start of at least four weeks regardless of what the series does.

**Effect on the reported figures.** Registered rule, reproduced on this vintage: mean divergence lead over peak 13.63 wk (paper 14.148; difference is data vintage), mean level lead 5.926 wk (paper 5.926, exact), advantage 7.70 wk, divergence first in 26 of 27 (exact), minimum lead 5 wk (exact). Boundary seasons vs the rest: advantage 9.2 wk in the 10 boundary seasons, 7.5 wk in the other 16. Under a **strict crossing rule** (divergence must move from ≤ 0.2 to > 0.2 inside the window): scorable in 18 of 27 seasons; mean divergence lead 12.94 wk vs level 5.78 wk; advantage 7.17 wk; divergence first in 18 of 18. Season 2025-26: registered rule wk40 vs level wk48 (8-wk lead, as shown on the live board); strict crossing rule wk47 vs wk48 (1 wk). Season 1997-98: the divergence is undefined until week 51 (series start), so its recorded onset (wk51) and the level comparator's earlier firing (wk47) are a data-start artifact; it is the single "level-first" season in the 26-of-27 count.

**Change made.** The mechanism claim stands (divergence still first in every scorable season under the strict rule) but the headline "8.222 weeks" (abstract, §5.1.2, C-03) is inflated by roughly one week by boundary firings and must be read alongside the crossing-rule figure of 7.17 wk. No `claims.lock` entry is altered; this is a disclosure. `verify.py` unaffected. **Registration addendum, dated before MMWR week 40 data exists:** for 2026-27 a secondary *crossing onset* readout (first in-window week at which D moves from ≤ 0.2 to > 0.2) is added to the live board and to `PREDICTIONS_TRACKER.md`, reported beside P1. P1 is scored exactly as registered; if D is above 0.2 at week 40 and never dips, the crossing readout is recorded as "not scorable" and any P1 pass is flagged *boundary-assisted*. The registered rule is not changed mid-test.

**Reproduction.** `verification/flu_onset_boundary_diagnostic.py` (MD5 cbfda6f8557cd2ce4ddf8260fdea1533) regenerates every figure above; per-season table `verification/flu_onset_boundary_diagnostic.csv` (MD5 bf7c0647da5cacc5338b0e52e71fb710); pinned pull `verification/ilinet_nat_pull_2026-09-30.json` (MD5 67e5ee1ea4fb57ab550c0ac9c7f0d16e).

**Who identified it.** The author, on noticing that the live divergence (0.312 at 2026w36) was above the trigger before the window opened and that the prior season had fired on its first eligible week.
