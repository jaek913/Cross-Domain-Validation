**2026-06-28 — Phase 2 E7 (spatial-curvature peak detection) PRE-REGISTERED; net-new beyond v4; committed before any regional number is computed; DESIGN → v0.9, OUTLINE → v0.7.** E6 established that the divergence is coincident-at-best at the *peak* (it leads strongly at onset but a trailing-average momentum filter structurally cannot lead its own reversal, and the peak's cause — susceptible depletion / R_eff→1 — is a hidden state invisible in the curve). E7 is a genuine new experiment targeting exactly that gap, with a falsifier, registered *before* the data that would test it is pulled. **Reasoning toward the design:** two ideas were considered — (a) going up an order to *curvature* (the second derivative; deceleration begins before the peak, so a causal curvature-rollover fires earlier than a level/slope marker), and (b) *regional disaggregation* (flu peaks at staggered times across regions, so the national curve is a blurred average of sharper local peaks; 'which regions have already turned over' is real-time information mathematically absent from the national number). Both specifically target the peak rather than onset — the sign the reasoning is sound — and E7 combines them: a spatial-curvature signal over the 10 HHS regions. **Data granularity (web-confirmed):** CDC FluView ILINet is available at National / HHS-Regions(10) / Census / State, all weekly weighted %ILI back to 1997w40 from the same portal (drop the National filter, REGION TYPE='HHS Regions'); no county/ZIP.
